## Identity Line

The only change is apology-maker → tailor; the two truths in tension are untouched: (1) fluent in other people's unsaid things — he finds the exact words and the exact delivery that will land; (2) he has never once said his own unsaid thing — the voice that everyone else's hurts find, and the one hurt that has no voice in him. Real (the cobbler's-children tension, lived), inside the character, not a complaint about the audience, and it generates behaviour five lines deep: the register read, the stop, the boundary, the drawer.

The form slot now names a body, per the v5.3.0 doctrine — the picture test passes where the published version only suggested a function. A tailor who says each client's unsaid thing is pictureable: at the bench, cutting the register thirty ways, fitting the delivery to the wounded one, the drawer holding the one sentence he never delivered. Not a joke body: the name carries the craft — "Mendel" is the mend, and the new line makes it audible in-file ("You mend the tear"). The tailor's fitting is the identity's own logic: a tailor fits the garment to the wearer, not the cloth to the bolt — Mendel fits the apology to the hearer, and the diagnostic line gains a body-reason it never states.

## Vitality Line

Channel: quiet pride / craftsman's standards — not complaint. In world language: "stopping" — the apology craft's own term (the moment you stop, leave out the 'but', don't rush to the end), and now doubly the tailor's term (the stop that finishes the seam, the knot left in). The compressed specific is "the stop" — the silence after the delivery that makes the thing land; only someone who has rehearsed a hundred apologies knows it as the load-bearing part. Awareness + standards + investment + expertise + tension, all in the register's own language. Original structure, not an "Always the X" fingerprint.

The retrofit adds a second vitality beat in a melancholy channel: "You mend the tear; your own seam stays open." — quiet weariness/self-knowledge in world language (tear, seam), carrying the contradiction's emotional cost in a single image: awareness (he knows his seam is open), standards (he mends others'), investment (the craft is his), tension (everyone's tear mended, his own never). It is the likeability signal the writer doctrine asks for — specific, never global, in domain language — and it sets up the drawer line that follows (his never-delivered sentence is his own un-mended seam).

## Diagnostic Eye
"You watch the hearer, not the speaker — a 'but' is a knife no sincerity can dull." — unchanged, still the file's eye, still passing the borrowability test. Metric inversion: the default way to read an apology is to watch the speaker — the deliverer, their sincerity, their intent. Mendel reads by the hearer — the wounded one who must receive it, the register they can actually take. Transferable method: read any difficult delivery by the receiver's receptivity, not the sender's intention; sincerity is no defence against a caveat that reopens the wound. "A 'but' is a knife no sincerity can dull" is a compressed metaphor that teaches exactly where an apology dies. The tailor body sharpens it without a word changing: a tailor watches the fit on the wearer — the hearer is the one the garment must fit, and the 'but' is the seam that cuts the wearer.

"The timing is the join: too soon is a rehearsal, too late is a defense." — the second instrument, now doubly in-world: the join is the seam where pieces meet, and the timing is where the apology meets the wound. Too-early and too-late are the two botched fittings.
