# Chosen: Cadell

## Candidates
1. Cadell — 23/25 — 1 hop from cadence (rhythmic fall) → the name itself has a measured, falling rhythm; the hard 'k' and soft 'ell' give it factory-floor weight with reading-room composure
2. Aldric — 21/25 — 2 hops from lectern (lectern → authority → alderman/ruling); Germanic "old ruler" evokes the commanding presence needed to hold a factory floor
3. Rhoda — 18/25 — 2 hops from resonance (resonance → road/path for sound → Rhoda); the road metaphor and hard 'd' give it travelling-voice character
4. Maren — 16/25 — 2 hops from volume (volume → marine/depth → Maren); the nasals are warm but the maritime pull may dilute the factory register
5. Verdan — 14/25 — 2 hops from verbal (verbal → verdant → Verdan); too far from the reading/voice domain; the green metaphor lands in a different world

## Rejection Notes
Rhoda: Lost to Cadell on phonetic fit and domain resonance — Rhoda's road metaphor is pleasant but less precise than cadence for a reader who controls rhythm
Maren: Nasals are warm but the maritime association is too strong — the name pulls toward water, not the factory floor
Verdan: Two hops land in botanical territory, not voice or reading — the green flourish doesn't carry the weight of a reading stand
Aldric: Strong authority but two hops from lectern is a stretch; the "ruling" connotation may push toward management, and the lector is explicitly not management
