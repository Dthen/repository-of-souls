# Chosen: Calder

## Candidates
1. Calder — 19/25 — 1 hop from smoker (caldron); hard 'k' gives weight, 'er' softens; "rocky water" etymology
2. Rowan — 19/25 — 1 hop from propolis (tree resin → rowan bark); balanced warmth and groundedness
3. Thorn — 19/25 — 1 hop from sting (nature's parallel defense); sharp, memorable, but too sharp for the calm element
4. Linden — 19/25 — 1 hop from honey (linden honey); soft, flowing, but leans pastoral
5. Hazel — 18/25 — 2 hops from hive (hazel wood → skep construction); warm but too gentle for the danger

## Rejection Notes
Rowan: Strong balanced name, but the propolis→rowan connection requires knowing that propolis comes from tree resin — slightly less direct than Calder's smoker link. Loses the tiebreaker on phonetic weight.
Thorn: Most memorable candidate (5/5), but phonetically too sharp — leans entirely toward the danger side. The beekeeper archetype needs both strength AND calm; Thorn has no warmth. Also, 1 hop from sting puts it dangerously close to the center.
Linden: Lowest collision risk (5/5) and strong domain resonance through linden honey, but too soft — the flowing vowels evoke the pastoral trope the seed warns against. Fails to carry the weight of someone who opens a hive of ten thousand stingers.
Hazel: Warm and natural, 2 hops from hive via hazel-wood skeps. But too gentle — reads as the "gentle beekeeper" pastoral trope the functional risk explicitly warns against. No consonant weight.
