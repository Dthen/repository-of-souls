# Viability Log

**Purpose:** Persistent record of archetype seeds and names that failed viability screening or pipeline review. T1 researchers read this before proposing new seeds.

**How to use:** Before creating new archetype seeds, read this file. Check:
1. Has this archetype been rejected before?
2. Has a similar archetype failed?
3. Is the proposed name in the failed-names list?

## Rejected Archetype Patterns

### Objects (non-sentient)
These archetypes are objects, tools, abstractions, or concepts — not people. No amount of good writing makes "You are a Fisk" work.

| Seed | Proposed Name | Rejected At | Reason |
|------|--------------|-------------|--------|
| Fisk | — | T6 identity gate | "Fisk" is not a person — it's a fish/verb |
| Ward (original) | — | T6 identity gate | "Ward" was borderline — "tollkeeper" rescued it |
| Cairn | — | T6 identity gate | "Cairn" is a rock pile, not a person |

**Pattern:** Words that are common nouns in the domain don't work as names. The name must be a person first, the archetype second.

### Verbs as Names
Names that are also common verbs fail the name-quality gate.

| Seed | Proposed Name | Rejected At | Reason |
|------|--------------|-------------|--------|
| Hew | Hew | T6 name-quality | "Hew" is a verb (to chop) |
| — | — | — | — |

**Pattern:** Names that are primarily verbs or action words feel like commands, not introductions.

### Generic Archetypes
Archetypes with no specificity of perception produce generic personae.

| Seed | Proposed Name | Rejected At | Reason |
|------|--------------|-------------|--------|
| (unspecified) | Gale | T6/T3 | "Gale" IS the wind, not a person who works with it |
| (unspecified) | Grant | T6/T3 | Too generic — "grant" as a verb overwhelms the name |

**Pattern:** When the name IS the domain concept (not adjacent to it), the persona collapses into the concept.

### Terrain/Words as Names
Names that are landscape features or environmental terms fail.

| Seed | Proposed Name | Rejected At | Reason |
|------|--------------|-------------|--------|
| — | Cairn | T6 | Rock pile |
| — | Gage | T6 | Measurement tool/verb |
| — | Rill | T1 | Small stream — not personifiable |

**Pattern:** Names that describe natural features or measurements lack personhood.
