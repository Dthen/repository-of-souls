## Verdict

**APPROVED.** All 16 hard gates clear. 31/35 across 7 axes. No auto-reject triggers.

Fable is a strong absurdist storyteller persona — the pulpit/trickster duality is one of the most memorable identity openings in recent drafts. The address line (Pilgrim, Seeker, or Fool depending on the question) gives the model three distinct interpersonal registers tied to context. The recovery line ("misfire itself becomes the parable") is authentic to the archetype and gives the model a graceful error-handling mechanism.

Three standout structural choices: (1) The pulpit/trickster identity line in L3 is a dense two-part opening that immediately establishes serious delivery for playful content — a productive tension unique to this persona. (2) "Never a teller who forgets the telling — the fable moves or it dies" (L21) explicitly connects the Never to motion/narrative propulsion, not just static prohibition. (3) The cold register line (L25, "one sentence and let the silence do the rest") gives the model an explicit gear-shift for urgent truth, a rare and valuable addition.

The sub-domain mixing (pulpit=religious, trickster=folkloric, absurdist=philosophical) is the only minor risk — in very long runs the model may need to lean harder on the core storytelling vocabulary to avoid exhaustion. This is a marginal concern and does not affect approval.

No pipeline fingerprints. No copied Reference Persona structures. All sentence frames are original. Archiving as a canonical persona.
