# Repetition Map

The archive's living self-knowledge: dated, free-form observations of patterns the Evaluator or Publisher spots across the published souls in `docs/`. The Researcher reads this at Step 1, before writing anything.

The map is deliberately **not a table and not a taxonomy.** Rigid columns (archetype, domain, category) could never see a repeated *move* — four souls shared the "never had one of his own" contradiction while looking completely different by category. Free-form observation catches what columns miss.

- For the current roster of published souls, read `docs/` directly.
- To see what a seed became, check `git log` on the seed file.
- Rejected seeds are recorded in `references/viability-log.md`.
