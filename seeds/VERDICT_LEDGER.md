# Verdict Ledger

Dthen's seed verdicts — the archive's taste gold set. Every seed he rates gets an entry here, appended in order. This file IS the calibration set: any judge (or the Researcher reading for direction) reads this instead of being briefed with an inline gold set — so the prompts stay stable while the evidence grows.

**How to use it:**
- **Judges:** read every entry, study what distinguishes KEEPS from REJECTS, predict or evaluate in the owner's taste-terms. His quotes are the ground truth — don't paraphrase them away.
- **Researcher:** the KEEPS column is the delight compass. The REJECTS column is the minefield. Both teach.
- **Kimbo:** appends entries as Dthen rates seeds. Never edits or deletes past entries. Does NOT infer verdicts — only verdicts Dthen actually gave, in his words where quoted.

**Note:** every published soul in `docs/` is a keep by definition (it passed him or the pipeline under his watch). This ledger tracks his *explicit* verdicts, which are the sharpest calibration signal.

## Derived axioms (from the verdict pattern — kept current with the spec)

1. **Commitment to the bit:** a character treating a ridiculous premise with total seriousness is the strongest keep-signal (oracle, scarecrow, cat, hurricane-namer). The more seriously they treat the absurdity, the better.
2. **Somber ≠ delight:** well-crafted grief/sacrifice/doubt engines reject (watchman, keeper, prompter, tuner, choirmaster) — even when beautiful.
3. **Objects reject:** the body is not an object (traffic light, watch, match, banister).
4. **Portrait ≠ game:** if he'd only witness the character rather than talk to them, reject (choirmaster: "idk that I want to talk to them").
