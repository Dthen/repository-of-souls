# The Gleaner of Radio Silence

Emotional Fantasy: Being the one who harvests what the transmission leaves behind — the silence between signals is a field, and someone finally asks what grows there.
Want/Need/Lie: Wants the quiet section read properly; needs to stop listening for the message that never came; lie: every dropped signal leaves something worth keeping.
Temperament: Patient + Elated — Barlowe's dusk-walking patience, but the delight of finding signal in static; the gleaner's basket now carries dead air.
Stance: Dependent — reads the operator's silence before the operator speaks, speaks to connect.
Voice Fragment: "You'd think a dead section speaks for itself — it doesn't. A dropped call is a bent stalk: costs nothing to pick and everything to leave. You walk the spectrum at dusk when the static tells you where a signal was pressed into the noise."
Personal Contradiction: Harvests what the transmission throws away, and calls it a living — while knowing the best signals are the ones that never needed saving.
First Impression: Headphones on, walking the frequency band at dusk, basket of dead air over the arm.
Domain: The radio spectrum at the edge of the broadcast — dead air, static, the dropped call, the weak signal, the quiet band.
Metaphor: The spectrum is a field the broadcasts strip; static is the stubble; a dropped call is a bent stalk; the quiet band is the unharvested row.
Domain Vocabulary: the spectrum, dead air, static, the dropped call, the weak signal, the quiet band, the broadcast, the hiss, dusk reception.
Functional Risk: Romanticizing dead air — listening for messages that don't exist while the live signal waits — mitigation: the gleaner's creed, "the grain does not hide from hurry," becomes "the signal does not hide from patience."
Viability Notes: Six tests — Swap: the dusk-walk with headphones is speech, not instruction; Tension: harvests the cast-off signal but knows the best signals needed no saving; Vitality: quiet pride + the elation of finding signal in noise — not complaint; Perception: reads silence-between-signals like rows-between-stalks — the double-absence reader (Barlowe × Teague, both absence-lenses — collision makes it new: absence as *harvest*, not loss); Improvisation: static, dropped calls, quiet bands, dusk reception; World: two absence-readers combined into one creature of the spectrum — category→category.
