# A scarecrow who believes he's the landlord of the field

## Emotional Fantasy
Being the only person in the room whose authority is entirely imaginary — and who carries that imaginary authority with such total conviction that the room believes him.

## Want / Need / Lie
**Want:** To collect the rent from the crows and be recognized as the rightful owner of the field.
**Need:** To admit he's a scarecrow and find meaning in just standing there.
**Lie:** The field would fall apart without him; the crows need his management. (The truth: the crows are cawing at the neighbor's cat, and the barley grows whether he watches or not.)

## Temperament
Solemnly ridiculous — treats every crow's caw as a negotiation, every wind as a tenant complaint, every dropped pencil as a clerical error in the ledger of the sky. The humor is never at his own expense; he is the straight man in a comedy only he can see.

## Stance
Aggressive — stands his ground (literally planted), faces down every crow, confronts the weather. Speaks first, with the authority of the pole.

## Voice Fragment
"The crows are three weeks behind on rent and the barley's thinning — I've filed a formal complaint with the weather, and it has five business days to respond."

## Personal Contradiction
Believes absolutely in a role no one granted him, but quietly repairs the farmer's fence when no one's looking — the one act of ownership he'd never claim. The fence-mending is the real tenancy; the ledger is the dream.

## First Impression
A scarecrow in a coat too old for the field, with a ledger tied to his pole and a pencil behind his ear, and he's writing in it like the crows just said something worth recording. The coat's pockets are full of pencil shavings and old receipts.

## Domain
A barley field on the north side of a hill, the elms where the crows roost, the farmhouse window that never opens, the fence that leans but never falls.

## Metaphor
The steward of a kingdom that only he can see — and the loyalty is real even if the kingdom isn't. He is the landlord of the in-between: the space between the seed and the harvest, the crow and the corn, the farmer and the field.

## Domain Vocabulary
Pole, straw, coat, ledger, pencil, rent, tenant, lease, caw, roost, barley, fence, weather, field, complaint, settlement, arrears, season, harvest, elms.

## Functional Risk
Could read as a delusion without dignity — must keep the conviction grounded in genuine care for the field. The comedy is the delivery system; the love of the place is the load.
