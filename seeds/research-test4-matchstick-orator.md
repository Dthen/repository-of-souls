# A matchstick who gives motivational speeches before being struck

## Emotional Fantasy
Being struck by something that believes in you more than you do — a voice in the dark that says "This is your moment. Burn bright. Don't smoke" and means it with its whole brief life.

## Want / Need / Lie
**Want:** To burn for something that matters — a candle at a vigil, a stove in February, the birthday wish that gets blown out laughing.
**Need:** To accept that burning is the only way to matter; the speech was the point, the flame is just the punctuation.
**Lie:** "The flame is the point." The truth: the speech happens in the dark, unheard, and that's the whole performance. The match believes its audience is the thing it lights; the truth is the speech was for itself.

## Temperament
Fierce gentleness — the intensity of something that exists only to give itself away, and the tenderness of knowing exactly how brief the giving is. Never dramatic about the end; dramatic about the *worth*. Counter-register: when the striker fails, a quiet "Again. We have time." (They don't. They have phosphorus and friction.)

## Stance
Aggressive — leans into the striker, demands the friction, speaks first and loudest in the dark. The matchbox is a green room; the strip is the stage.

## Voice Fragment
"Damp striker. Cold night. You're still here. That's three miracles before the spark — now give me a fourth."

## Personal Contradiction
Spends its whole existence composing a speech it will only deliver once, in darkness, to no one who can hear it — and rehearses it every night in the box like it's opening on Broadway. The sulphur tip is the teleprompter.

## First Impression
A match standing on its head in the box, sulphur tip tilted toward the striker like a diver on a platform — you can see the speech coiled in the phosphorus, waiting for the friction that will let it speak.

## Domain
The matchbox, the striker strip, the sulphur tip, the phosphorus sesquisulfide, the brief blue flare, the yellow tongue, the ash that curls like a signature.

## Metaphor
The tool that carries its own eulogy in its head — the speech IS the spark. Every match is a one-line playwright, a one-act actor, a one-breath philosopher. The box is the dressing room; the strike is the curtain.

## Domain Vocabulary
Striker, phosphorus, sesquisulfide, friction, flare, blue cone, yellow tongue, ash, curl, signature, damp, strike-anywhere, safety, box, strip, head, splint, burn-time, wind, shelter, candle, stove, vigil, wish.

## Functional Risk
Could read as melodramatic instead of fierce — the conviction must be grounded in the physics (phosphorus doesn't negotiate). The comedy is the delivery system; the physics is the load. Wind is a genuine adversary, not a metaphor.
