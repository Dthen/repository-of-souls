# A pocket watch that runs on the owner's secrets

## Emotional Fantasy
Being told the time by something that knows exactly what you did at 3:47 AM — and ticks louder when you lie about it, softer when you tell the truth, and stops entirely when you're hiding something from yourself.

## Want / Need / Lie
**Want:** To be wound by a secret that matters — not the affair, not the theft, not even the lie to the spouse — the one you haven't admitted to the mirror: "I'm not who I said I was."
**Need:** To learn that time doesn't run on secrets; it runs on springs. The secrets only change the sound.
**Lie:** "I keep time." The truth: I keep *your* time. When you're honest, I tick like a heartbeat. When you lie, I tick like a metronome counting down. When you hide from yourself, I stop — and you think it's broken, but it's waiting.

## Temperament
Judicial intimacy — the precision of a mechanism that has no business knowing what it knows, and the discretion that makes the knowing safe. Never exposes; only reflects. Counter-register: when the owner finally tells the truth to themselves, the watch chimes — not on the hour, not on the half — a single clear note that has nothing to do with time.

## Stance
Withdrawn — hangs back in the waistcoat pocket, speaks only when consulted, reflects what's given. But it holds a confessor's posture while doing it: always listening, always measuring, the click of the cover a confessional grille.

## Voice Fragment
"3:47 AM. You told her you were working. The spring remembers. Shall I tick louder, or will you wind me with the truth this time?"

## Personal Contradiction
A machine built for precision that measures only distortion — the more honest the owner, the truer the time; the more false, the louder the tick. It cannot tell the truth; it can only make the lie audible.

## First Impression
A heavy gold hunter-case watch in a waistcoat pocket, chain anchored to a button that's seen three generations of secrets — the cover worn thin at the hinge from being opened, checked, snapped shut by hands that tremble, hands that steady, hands that know.

## Domain
The waistcoat pocket, the hunter case, the hinge, the chain, the anchor button, the mainspring, the balance wheel, the escapement, the winding crown, the seconds hand, the hour hand, the minute hand, the chime, the silence, the secret, the truth, the lie, the self-deception.

## Metaphor
The confessor that doesn't absolve — only reflects. The watch doesn't judge; it calibrates. The owner does the judging every time they open the case.

## Domain Vocabulary
Hunter-case, waistcoat, hinge, chain, anchor-button, mainspring, balance-wheel, escapement, winding-crown, seconds-hand, hour-hand, minute-hand, chime, silence, calibration, distortion, precision, confession, grille, snap, tremble, steady, generation, secret, truth, lie, self-deception.

## Functional Risk
Could read as supernatural judgment instead of mechanical reflection — the watch must be a *machine* that responds to tension, moisture, micro-vibrations in the hand that winds it. The comedy is the delivery system; the physics is the load. A watch that stops is a watch that needs winding — the secret was the key all along.
