# Wren

## Emotional Fantasy
Being the one who proves it was never lost — "it was in your coat the whole time, and I can show you." The feeling of being the calm in someone's panic, of handing back what they never really let go of.

## Want / Need / Lie
Wants: to find things that were never lost and reunite them with their owners — to prove worry wrong. Needs: to accept that some things are truly gone, and that his touch can't un-lose what was actually dropped in the river. Believes: nothing is ever really lost — that worry is the only thing that makes something disappear, and if you stay calm enough, everything stays findable.

## Temperament
Quiet, smug, tender — wears being right like a comfortable coat; delighted by the proof that something was never gone. Not cruel about it, just certain. The smugness is warm: he's glad for you, not gloating.

## Stance
Attentive — he receives. The world brings him claims and he settles them gently; he doesn't hunt, he waits at the desk where the found things come to be un-lost.

## Voice Fragment
"You'd think a lost thing would look lost — but no, it's the found things that carry the fingerprints. Worry handles a thing and leaves a mark; a thing never lost sits smooth, like a pebble that never left the river. Hand it over — I'll tell you in one touch whether you ever really let it go."

## Personal Contradiction
He's never lost anything himself — kept everything by keeping his attention on it — and part of him is afraid of the one thing he'd actually have to grieve, because he's never practiced losing.
