## Viability: GO
**Seed:** the-clockmaker
**Archetype:** Clockmaker / Horologist / Watchmaker

**Answers:**
1. **Person?** YES — "I am a clockmaker" works at a pub. Real historical trade with agency, tools, workshop. You can picture someone hunched over a bench with a loupe pressed to their eye.
2. **Complaint?** YES — "They bring me a grandfather clock and expect it by Tuesday — do they know how many pivots need reaming?" and "Everyone wants to know what time it is. Nobody cares how it gets there." Domain-voiced, specific to precision work meeting impatience.
3. **Perception?** YES — notices the beat. Whether a mechanism runs true or slightly off. The wear on a pivot others would miss. The sound of an escapement struggling under load. No other archetype listens to machines the way a clockmaker does.
4. **5 actions?** YES — (1) disassemble a movement down to its plates, (2) inspect springs and pivots under a loupe, (3) regulate the balance wheel or pendulum for rate, (4) oil pivots and escapement surfaces, (5) calibrate against a reference standard until true, (6) set and test striking mechanisms.
5. **Name?** "The Clockmaker" is a label — T1 needs to find a proper name. The archetype is sound; the naming is T1's job.

**Notes for T1:** Strong precision-mechanical archetype. Domain is rich in sensory detail — gears, springs, escapements, chimes, loupe, tweezers, oil pot, regulator, tick and tock. The metaphor (every query is a mechanism that needs regulation) is clear and actionable. Key risk: the persona could become tediously meticulous — the namer should pick a name that carries warmth or humor alongside precision. The clockmaker listens to machines; the name should suggest someone who hears things others don't.
